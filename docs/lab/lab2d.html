How the CoRe-AE head renders one voxel (2-D view)

An axial CT slice with the latent grid on top (one latent cell = 4 × 4 voxels, r = 4). Drag the query point c. The feature grid u lives on the cells; the head turns u into intensities at any coordinate. In 3-D, LIIF reads 8 neighbouring cells instead of the 4 shown here.
